Urban Luxury in
Edgewater

Art Plaza Tower is a luxury apartment community at 58 NE 14th Street in Miami’s Edgewater neighborhood — in the heart of the Arts and Entertainment District. Residences are finished with granite counter tops, stainless steel appliances, a double door refrigerator, laminated wood floors in living rooms, porcelain tiles in bathrooms, and full-size washer and dryer machines.

Directly across from the School Board Metromover Station, you are connected to Downtown, Brickell, Midtown, and the rest of Miami without leaving the block. A large swimming pool, fully equipped fitness center, social center, and covered parking complete the lifestyle.
